What to try
  1. Pick each project; check the schematic and the PCB tab render.
  2. Click / tap a symbol or footprint → its reference appears above (this becomes @REF in chat).
  3. Pan (drag / one finger) and zoom (wheel / pinch). Is it smooth?
  4. PCB: click a track or zone. Known issue: net highlight throws on KiCad 11 files (nets by name; not patched) — the demo shows an amber notice.
  5. complex_hierarchy / video: double-click / double-tap a sheet box to enter it; use the Sheet list to go back.
  6. Type a reference (e.g. U1) into “Highlight REF”.
  7. Toggle Light/Dark (top right). Note: boards stay dark (KiCanvas has no light board theme).
  8. jetson-agx-thor PCB (85 MB) is the stress test — watch load time and pan/zoom.
  9. On the phone: rotate, tap small parts, try pinch zoom.
